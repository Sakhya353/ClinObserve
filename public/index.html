Modules in detail

What each module tracks

The core workflow is Patient → Appointment → Consultation → Observation → Medication Record → Follow-up. Each stage is its own searchable, filterable module.

👁 Observation

Operational observation records linked to each consultation.

  • Types: Routine, Follow-up, Administrative, Monitoring, Review, Attention Required
  • Synthetic vitals: heart rate, blood pressure, temperature, SpO₂, respiratory rate
  • Status and follow-up requirement
  • “Attention Required” is an operational label, never a diagnosis

📋 Consultation

Every visit tracked from booking to outcome.

  • Types: OPD, Routine, Follow-up, Review, Observation
  • Status: Scheduled, Waiting, In Consultation, Completed, Follow-up, Cancelled
  • Check-in, start and end times
  • Waiting time and consultation duration

💊 Medication

Historical synthetic medication records only.

  • Medication, dosage, frequency and route
  • Start and end dates
  • Status: Active, Completed, Discontinued, Historical
  • No recommendations or dosage advice, ever

FAQ

Common questions

Is this a real hospital system?

No. ClinObserve is a synthetic research and portfolio prototype inspired by large tertiary-care workflows. It is not an official AIIMS Delhi system.

Does it use real patient data?

Never. All names, IDs, observations and medication records are generated and fictional.

Can it diagnose or recommend treatment?

No. It provides no diagnosis, treatment, medication or dosage advice, and its AI assistant is limited to operational summaries.

What is the AI assistant for?

It summarizes consultation activity, waiting-time patterns, department trends and medication-record statistics, and can generate operational reports.

Who is it for?

Data analysts, researchers and students exploring healthcare operations analytics with safe, synthetic data.

Security

Built with production habits

Layered protections designed into the architecture from day one.

  • JWT authentication with bcrypt-hashed passwords
  • Role-based access: Admin, Doctor, Observer, Ops, Analyst
  • Helmet, CORS and rate limiting on every API route
  • Zod input validation and sanitized error responses
  • Environment-based secrets, none committed to Git
  • Full audit log of user activity
Synthetic-data ethics

No real patients. No real data. Ever.

Every name, ID, observation and medication record is fictional and generated for demonstration. ClinObserve does not diagnose, recommend treatment or advise on medication. It is a research and portfolio prototype, not an official AIIMS Delhi system.
